# Code of Conduct

Zikr is a small app that people use as part of their worship.
Contributors come from different countries, languages, and schools of
thought. Please treat everyone here with that in mind.

## Expected

- Be kind, patient, and assume good intent.
- Keep feedback about the work, not the person.
- When a question is about religious content (wording, sources,
  translations), cite your source and respect that scholars differ.
  Zikr sticks to well-known adhkar from Hisn al-Muslim to stay out of
  disputed areas. Please help keep it that way.
- Welcome newcomers and people writing in their second or third
  language.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or personal attacks.
- Mocking anyone's faith, sect, ethnicity, gender, or language.
- Sectarian arguments or takfir.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Spam, self-promotion, or repeated off-topic posting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues or PRs, and
block accounts that break these rules. To report a problem privately,
use the contact on the maintainer's GitHub profile
([@shamsbd71](https://github.com/shamsbd71)) or GitHub's
[report abuse](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
tools. Reports are kept confidential.

This code applies in the repository, its issues, pull requests, and
discussions, and anywhere you represent the project.
